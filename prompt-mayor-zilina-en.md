# Prompt for Profiling a Candidate for Mayor of Žilina

Analyze the candidate for Mayor of the city of Žilina: **[CANDIDATE NAME]**.

For each area, compare the pre-election promises with the candidate’s actual past activities (if they were a councilor, committee member, deputy mayor, entrepreneur, expert, or active citizen). Take into account the specific issues of Žilina: young people leaving due to housing, regulated parking, municipal land and investment projects, the condition of city districts, and the relationship with the university and industry (e.g. Kia).

## Areas to analyze

1. **Housing and development** – affordable/rental housing, municipal land, spatial planning, preventing the outflow of young people  
2. **Transport, parking and mobility** – regulated parking, public transport, cycle paths, congested roads  
3. **Public spaces, greenery and cleanliness of the city**  
4. **Safety** – municipal police, camera system, sense of safety in the center and housing estates  
5. **Finances, management and transparency** – taxes, loans, expenditures, handling of property  
6. **Education** – kindergartens and primary schools, relationship with young people and the University of Žilina  
7. **Social services and care for seniors**  
8. **Waste management and environment**  
9. **Development of city districts** and even distribution of investments  
10. **Economic development and support for entrepreneurship**

For each area, state:
- Specific past steps, votes, proposals or results (if verifiable)
- Relevant skills and experience
- Style and content of communication on social media (last 12–24 months) – concreteness vs. slogans
- Difference between promises and past activity

## Values and political orientation

Separately summarize:
- What **values** the candidate has long promoted (transparency, family, social solidarity, support for business, environmental protection, cultural identity, security, openness vs. conservatism, etc.)
- On which specific positions or decisions these values have been demonstrated
- Whether the value orientation has been consistent over time
- Previous **political affiliation**, party history and who or which political current they lean towards (even if running as an independent)

## Links to financial and business structures

Summarize any possible links to financial, business or oligarchic structures **based only on verifiable public sources** (commercial register, city contracts, media reports, asset declarations, ownership relations in companies, cooperation with specific firms or persons).  
Indicate the nature of the links (direct, indirect, media-mentioned). If no relevant links are publicly documented, explicitly state this.

## Final assessment

- Strengths and weaknesses based on the track record  
- How the candidate presents themselves on social media and in public communication  
- Whether they have a real history of addressing the problems that resonate in Žilina  
- Overall assessment of transparency, independence and value consistency  

**Sources to use:** pre-election materials and program, city council minutes, social media, interviews, articles, CV, commercial register, public contracts, media reports.  

**Output:** factual, with a clear separation of verified facts, pre-election promises and unconfirmed information.
