# Prompt for Comparing Candidates – Žilina / Žilina Region (Elections 2026)

Compare the following candidates:

**Candidates:**
- [NAME 1]
- [NAME 2]
- [NAME 3]
- (add more as needed)

**Position:** Mayor of Žilina / President of the Žilina Self-Governing Region  
(adjust as needed)

## Task

1. Search for publicly available information about each candidate (program, past activity, political history, social media, articles, minutes, commercial register).
2. Compare them in key areas relevant to the position.
3. Create clear outputs suitable for visualization.

## Comparison areas (adjust according to the position)

**For Mayor of Žilina:** housing, transport and parking, safety, finances and transparency, education, social services, environment, development of city districts, values, political orientation, connections.

**For Regional President:** roads and bridges, regional transport, healthcare, social services, secondary education, regional development, tourism, finances, values, political orientation, connections.

## Required outputs

### 1. Overview table
Create a markdown table with candidates in columns and areas in rows.  
In the cells, provide a brief assessment or key facts (not long texts).

### 2. Value / ideological spectrum
Place the candidates on simple spectra, for example:
- Economic (more regulation ↔ more market)
- Social / cultural (conservative ↔ progressive)
- Transparency and openness (low ↔ high)
- Priority: infrastructure ↔ social services ↔ development

Indicate their position both in words and numerically (e.g. 1–10).

### 3. Benchmark / radar (text version)
For each candidate, rate 5–7 key areas on a scale of 1–10 (based on available information and past activity, not just promises).  
Format the output so it can be easily redrawn into a radar chart (e.g. list of points).

### 4. Strengths and weaknesses (comparison)
Short overview: in which areas each candidate is stronger / weaker.

### 5. Connections and transparency
Briefly state whether there are publicly documented financial or business connections (only verifiable sources).

### 6. Final summary
2–4 sentences: main differences between the candidates and what to watch out for.

## Rules

- Separate verified facts from promises and interpretations.
- If information is missing, state it explicitly.
- Be factual and balanced.
- Make outputs clear and visually usable (tables, scales, lists).

**Sources:** publicly available information (programs, media, social media, minutes, commercial register, etc.).
