# Prompt for Profiling a Candidate for President of the Žilina Self-Governing Region

Analyze the candidate for President of the Žilina Self-Governing Region: **[CANDIDATE NAME]**.

For each area, compare the pre-election promises with the candidate’s actual past activities. Take into account the specific issues of the Žilina Region: condition of class II and III roads and bridges, regional hospitals, aging population and capacity of social services, regional differences (Kysuce, Orava, Liptov, Turiec), retaining young people and the potential of tourism.

## Areas to analyze

1. **Class II and III roads, bridges** and their reconstruction  
2. **Regional bus transport** and integrated transport  
3. **Healthcare** – regional hospitals, staff, financing, availability of outpatient care  
4. **Social services** – capacities, modernization of facilities, home care, aging  
5. **Secondary education** and dual education  
6. **Regional development, innovation** and retaining young people in the region  
7. **Tourism** and utilization of natural potential  
8. **Finances and management** of the region  
9. **Cooperation with municipalities and cities** and balancing regional differences  

For each area, state:
- Specific past steps, results or positions (if they were a regional councilor, mayor, in another function or an expert)
- Relevant skills and experience
- Style and content of communication on social media
- Difference between promises and past activity

## Values and political orientation

Separately summarize:
- What **values** the candidate has long promoted (transparency, regional balance, social solidarity, support for business, environmental protection, cultural identity, security, openness vs. conservatism, etc.)
- On which specific positions or decisions these values have been demonstrated
- Whether the value orientation has been consistent
- Previous **political affiliation**, party history and who or which political current they lean towards

## Links to financial and business structures

Summarize any possible links to financial, business or oligarchic structures **based only on verifiable public sources** (commercial register, regional contracts, media reports, asset declarations, ownership relations, cooperation with companies or persons).  
Indicate the nature of the links. If no relevant links are publicly documented, explicitly state this.

## Final assessment

- Strengths and weaknesses based on the track record  
- How the candidate communicates  
- Whether they have a history of addressing the most pressing problems of the region (roads, healthcare, social services)  
- Overall assessment of transparency, independence and value consistency  
- Possible regional preferences (Kysuce, Orava, Liptov, Turiec, etc.)  

**Sources to use:** pre-election materials, records from the regional assembly, social media, interviews, articles, CV, commercial register, public contracts, media reports.  

**Output:** factual, with a clear separation of verified facts, pre-election promises and unconfirmed information.
