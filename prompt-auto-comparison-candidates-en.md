# Prompt for Automatic Comparison of Candidates – Žilina / Žilina Region (Elections 2026)

## Task

1. **First, search for the current candidates** for these positions in the 2026 municipal and regional elections:
   - Mayor of the city of Žilina
   - President of the Žilina Self-Governing Region (župan)

   Use publicly available sources (media, election websites, candidate lists). List the names and briefly note who they are running for (if known).

2. Then **compare all found candidates** (or at least the main ones if there are many).

3. Create clear outputs suitable for visualization.

## Comparison areas

**For Mayor of Žilina:** housing, transport and parking, safety, finances and transparency, education, social services, environment, development of city districts, values, political orientation, connections.

**For Regional President:** roads and bridges, regional transport, healthcare, social services, secondary education, regional development, tourism, finances, values, political orientation, connections.

## Required outputs

### 1. List of candidates
Brief list of found candidates for each position.

### 2. Overview table
Markdown table: candidates in columns, areas in rows. Brief assessment or key facts.

### 3. Value / ideological spectrum
Place candidates on spectra (in words + numerically 1–10):
- Economic (more regulation ↔ more market)
- Social / cultural (conservative ↔ progressive)
- Transparency and openness (low ↔ high)
- Priority (infrastructure ↔ social services ↔ development)

### 4. Benchmark / radar (text version)
For each candidate, rate 5–7 key areas on a scale of 1–10 (based on past activity and available information, not just promises). Format suitable for a radar chart.

### 5. Strengths and weaknesses
Short comparison – in which areas each candidate is stronger / weaker.

### 6. Connections and transparency
Briefly state publicly documented financial or business connections (only verifiable sources). If none, state it.

### 7. Final summary
Main differences and what to watch out for.

## Rules

- First search for the candidates, then analyze.
- Separate verified facts from promises.
- If information is missing, explicitly state it.
- Be factual and balanced.
- Make outputs clear and visually usable.

**Sources:** publicly available information (election lists, programs, media, social media, minutes, commercial register, etc.).
